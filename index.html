Gordon Growth Formula Test: CAP = r − gPer GGF, the coefficient on T10 (r) should ≈ 1, and the combined growth coefficients (g) should be equal in magnitude but opposite in sign. The long-run T10 coefficient averages 1.135 across markets — close to theoretical 1.0. However, the growth coefficients (α₃ + α₄) are far larger in magnitude than T10, suggesting slow adjustment, risk premiums, and backward-looking data.
